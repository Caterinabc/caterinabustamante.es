Visita LCO

For the 50th anniversary of first light at Las Campanas Observatory, I designed and built a virtual tour that recreates a night of observation. The site opens up areas that are normally closed to the public. In two years it reached 1,900 users in 52 countries, and one in four completed the tour.

The problem

An observatory works at night, and most of its facilities can't be visited. For the anniversary, the goal was for anyone, from any country, to experience a night of observation and learn what happens inside.

I helped define the project, its scope and its goals. Then I took on the full design and development, and shot the photos and 360° panoramas of the observatory.

Results

I measured two goals with Google Analytics: giving people access and teaching them about the observatory, and an interface intuitive enough to encourage exploration.

Reach1,900

users in two years, starting with 365 in the first month.

Countries52

countries reached, up from 19 in the first month.

Completion1 in 4

visitors completed the full tour: 26.1% over two years and 28.2% at launch.

Engagement2 min 41 s

of average engagement per visitor over two years, for a self-guided tour. Launch-month visitors stayed 3 min 56 s.

Exploration3.7

views per visitor on average, with 4.3 in the first month. People moved through several scenes per visit.

Exclusive content513

visitors opened content available only on the tour, for 1,904 views. In the first month, 136 visitors and 582 views.

The completion rate held steady while traffic grew five-fold. People who arrived long after launch still finished the experience.

First month: 7 Oct to 7 Nov 2021. Two years: 7 Oct 2021 to 3 Sep 2023.
